# Retrieval Policy
Do not read papers by default.
Retrieve papers only when:
1. a term/object/theorem is unfamiliar;
2. a computational pattern suggests known literature;
3. a proof step requires an external theorem;
4. three consecutive attempts fail;
5. the problem clearly belongs to a specialized research area;
6. known constructions or bounds are needed.
Use this order:
topic card -> paper card -> theorem card -> relevant paper section -> full paper only if necessary.
Every source must be logged in retrieved_context.md.
