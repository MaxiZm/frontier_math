# Topic Card: Number Theory

## Scope
Properties of integers and algebraic numbers: primes and factorization, modular
arithmetic and congruences, Diophantine equations, algebraic number fields and
class groups, elliptic curves and modular forms, and L-functions.

## Key methods & tools
Reciprocity and congruence arguments; analytic estimates (sieves, zeta/L-function
asymptotics); algebraic number theory (rings of integers, ideal factorization);
integer-relation detection for recognizing constants. Typical tools: **PARI/GP**
(the specialist: factorization, `algdep`/`lindep`, L-functions, fast arithmetic),
**Sage** (number fields, elliptic curves, class groups), **mpmath** (high-
precision analytic evaluation).
