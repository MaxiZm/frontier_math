# Topic Card: Integrals and Special Functions

## Scope
Closed-form evaluation of definite integrals and sums, and the special functions
that arise: gamma/beta, hypergeometric `pFq`, Bessel, elliptic integrals,
polylogarithms, and zeta/L-values. Includes recognizing a numerical integral as a
closed form.

## Key methods & tools
Symbolic integration and summation; contour/residue methods; hypergeometric
representation and the WZ/creative-telescoping method; high-precision numerical
evaluation followed by constant recognition. Typical tools: **SymPy** (symbolic
integrals, series), **mpmath** (high-precision `quad`, special functions,
`identify`), **PARI/GP** (`algdep`/`lindep` to recognize the closed form), **Sage**
for symbolic-numeric bridges.
