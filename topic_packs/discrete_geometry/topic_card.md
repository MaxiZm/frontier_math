# Topic Card: Discrete Geometry

## Scope
Combinatorial and metric questions about finite point sets, lines, and convex
bodies: sphere packings and kissing numbers, incidence bounds
(Szemerédi–Trotter), distinct-distances problems, polytope combinatorics,
arrangements, and extremal configurations (equiangular lines, spherical codes).

## Key methods & tools
The polynomial method and incidence geometry; convexity and volume arguments;
linear-programming (Delsarte/Cohn–Elkies) bounds for packings; algebraic
constructions. Typical tools: **Sage** (exact algebra, lattices, polytopes),
**mpmath/PARI** (high-precision optimal-packing constants), **Z3/ILP** (finite
configuration search), **NetworkX** (incidence/intersection graphs).
