# Topic Card: Lattice Models

## Scope
Discrete statistical-mechanics models on lattices: the Ising and Potts models,
dimer and vertex models, percolation, self-avoiding walks, and their critical
exponents, partition functions, and exactly solvable cases.

## Key methods & tools
Transfer-matrix methods; exact solutions (Onsager, Yang–Baxter / commuting
transfer matrices); series expansions (high/low temperature) and Padé/extrapolation
for critical exponents; combinatorial enumeration of configurations. Typical
tools: **mpmath** (high-precision series and constants), **SymPy/Sage** (symbolic
transfer matrices, generating functions), **NetworkX** (lattice graph
construction), **PARI** (series acceleration and constant recognition).
