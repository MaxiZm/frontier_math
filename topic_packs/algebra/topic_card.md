# Topic Card: Algebra

## Scope
Structural algebra: groups, rings, fields, modules, and their representations;
polynomial rings and ideals (Gröbner bases); Galois theory; commutative algebra
and algebraic geometry; linear algebra over general fields.

## Key methods & tools
Structure theorems and homomorphism arguments; Gröbner-basis computation for
ideal membership, elimination, and solving polynomial systems; representation
theory and character tables; Galois-group computation. Typical tools: **Sage**
(polynomial rings, Gröbner bases, fields, Galois theory), **GAP** (finite groups,
representations, character tables), **SymPy** (symbolic linear algebra and
polynomials), **PARI** (number fields).
